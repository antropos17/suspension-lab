# Baku background art

The game uses a detailed offline raster panorama in `dist/assets/baku-panorama.png` (2172 x 724, 2,745,542 bytes). This replaces the earlier simplified vector skyline. It is original AI-generated architectural artwork, not a location photograph, measured reconstruction or exact street map. Landmark positions and relative scales are deliberately compressed; the Maiden Tower is enlarged for readability. Fine masonry, reflective curtain-wall panels and individual waterfront facades are baked into the texture.

## Visual research

- [HOK: Baku Flame Towers](https://www.hok.com/projects/view/baku-flame-towers/): the architect describes three curving flame-shaped towers on elevated ground, with a shared podium. This informed the separate curved glass silhouettes, facade panels and hill placement.
- [Icherisheher Reserve: Maiden Tower](https://icherisheher.gov.az/en/monuments/show/qiz-qalasi): a broad stone cylinder, 28/31 m high and 16.5 m in diameter, with a smooth lower portion and banded upper portion. The illustration includes weathered limestone, horizontal ribs, slit openings and a projecting masonry support. It is an artistic interpretation; the support and precise curvature are not a surveyed replica.
- [UNESCO: Walled City of Baku](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/958/): context for the limestone Old City, palace and tower ensemble.
- [Azerbaijan Tourism Board: Carpet Museum](https://azerbaijan.travel/visit-bakus-carpet-museum-cant-be-missed): the rolled-carpet form appears at the far right of the composite.

The sky is cool blue, limestone warm and glass silver-blue. The detailed city fills the middle distance; the darker physical car and road remain the playable foreground. Starting framing keeps both principal landmarks visible in the inspected narrow viewport. The foreground promenade has separate faster parallax. The clear upper sky strip extends to the top of the canvas without a rectangular image boundary. Alternate panorama tiles share mirrored edges; this is scenery continuity, not geographic continuation. Baku Eye is no longer represented in this revision.

## Runtime and storage

The PNG is loaded locally once and retained as one decoded image (about 6 MiB RGBA). One screen-sized wash canvas caches fallback sky/sea gradients. There is no per-frame building construction, remote image request, runtime generation or physics body for the background. Width and height follow the normal game canvas; no browser viewport setting is changed. Camera motion is deliberately slow to preserve landmark legibility along the 260 m course.

Generated using the built-in `imagegen` tool, then copied into the project without image edits. No new dependency or global configuration was installed. The original generation remains outside the project as tool output; the runtime consumes the project copy only.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: offline panoramic background texture for a side-view 2D driving game in Baku, Azerbaijan. Generate a very wide 3:1 landscape panorama, 3072 by 1024 if possible. Photoreal architectural photography, authentic Baku skyline from the Caspian seafront in clear late afternoon, neutral warm limestone with cool blue sky, no orange filter. Composition: perfectly level waterfront shoreline at 78 percent of image height, still blue-green sea in bottom 22 percent; pale blue sky above; all buildings fully contained. The main focal group, the THREE actual Flame Towers on the Highland Park hill, at x=55 to 73 percent of the width, must have correct smooth sculptural curved flame-shaped silhouettes, convex tapering silver blue glass curtain walls, fine rectangular window panels and reflective facets, three distinct curving towers not spikes, left one bending to the right and other two bending left, no extra towers, realistic podium and dense hillside city beneath them. At x=11 to 21 percent of width: the Maiden Tower / Qiz Qalasi in Icherisheher, 28m high and 16.5m diameter broad weathered tan limestone cylindrical tower with flat open circular roof and small parapet, characteristic tightly spaced horizontal projecting stone ribs on upper two thirds, smooth heavy stone courses in lower third, narrow staggered slit windows and a tall long thick projecting masonry buttress on its right, no domed roof or crenellations on Maiden Tower itself. The cityscape between them is dense original Baku oil-boom architecture: layered pale limestone French neoclassical waterfront mansions with pilasters, elaborate cornices, arched tall windows, wrought iron balconies, weathered masonry, varied slate and copper roofs, old city stone walls and a minaret. Small boulevard trees along water, minimal distant people. At far right the carpet museum rolled cylindrical gold facade can appear small. Highest towers at about 14 percent of image height, the Maiden Tower at 45 percent. Telephoto near-elevation panoramic view, realistic physical scale relationships compressed artistically to keep both landmarks legible, exquisite fine masonry and glass detail, natural atmospheric depth, believable photographic lighting. No text, no labels, no logos, no game car, no UI, no foreground road, no fantasy skyscrapers, no illustration or vector art. Keep panorama full bleed and its far left/right edges low unobtrusive waterfront buildings so continuation is easy.
